Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

FPL AI Pro ("we") is operated by [operator name]. This policy explains what we collect when you use the website or app, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where it applies, the UK/EU GDPR.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in (optional). Your email address and a Firebase user ID. We store your plan (free, founder, pro), plan expiry, subscription status and how many free briefings you've used each gameweek.
  • FPL team ID. When you import a team we fetch its public data from the official Fantasy Premier League API. We cache the result in server memory for a few minutes; we don't store it in a database.
  • Payments. Card details go directly to PayFast — we never see them. PayFast tells us the payment reference, amount, status and a subscription token so we can renew or cancel your plan.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting (Google Cloud / Firebase) records IP addresses, request paths and errors for security and debugging. Logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Who we share it with

  • Google — Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Run and Hosting (our processors), and the Gemini API: to write your weekly briefing we send it your squad's player names, injury news, the suggested captain and transfer, and your FPL team name. We don't send your email.
  • PayFast — your email and the plan you're buying, to process the subscription.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Some of these providers process data outside South Africa; they do so under their own data-protection terms.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove your record within 30 days, apart from payment records we must keep for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing [contact email]. You can also complain to the South African Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to cache the app for offline use. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

FPL AI Pro is not affiliated with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.
